# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. Projects should tailor the roles to their size and context; responsibilities may be combined or adapted when a role is not staffed separately.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor / Executive

### Role Summary
The Project Sponsor provides strategic sponsorship, confirms business priority and resources, and helps resolve escalated business decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Champion the project and confirm its alignment with organizational goals
- Secure or coordinate resources and provide timely business decisions
- Resolve escalated scope, priority, or resource trade-offs
- Review progress against intended outcomes and success measures

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with the Project Manager on escalations, constraints, and governance decisions
- Aligns with the Product Manager on business outcomes, priority, and changes to objectives
- Consults stakeholders and delivery leads when decisions affect their areas; does not replace the Product Manager's day-to-day backlog ownership

---

## Engineering Lead / Technical Lead

### Role Summary
The Engineering Lead guides technical delivery and helps the team make maintainable, secure, and feasible implementation choices.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate technical design and communicate architectural decisions
- Support estimation, capacity planning, and engineering sequencing
- Identify technical risks, quality needs, and cross-team dependencies
- Facilitate engineering collaboration and help resolve technical blockers

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with Developers on implementation, reviews, and technical problem-solving
- Partners with the Product Manager to assess feasibility and trade-offs, and with the Project Manager to surface dependencies and schedule risks
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on testability and quality strategy, and with the Release/Operations Owner on production readiness

---

## UX/UI Designer or User Researcher

### Role Summary
The UX/UI Designer or User Researcher brings user needs and usability evidence into product discovery, design, and validation.

### Responsibilities
- Plan or conduct appropriate user research and synthesize findings
- Create user flows, interaction designs, prototypes, or usability guidance
- Identify accessibility and usability considerations
- Help validate that delivered experiences address user needs

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with the Product Manager to connect research and design decisions to outcomes and priorities
- Works with Developers and the Engineering Lead to assess feasibility and preserve design intent
- Collaborates with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility checks, and engages stakeholders for relevant domain knowledge and feedback

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
The Business Analyst clarifies business processes and translates stakeholder needs into clear, traceable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business needs, workflows, and constraints
- Help refine requirements and acceptance criteria with examples and edge cases
- Trace requirements to project objectives and support scope clarification
- Identify process impacts and communicate requirement changes

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Works with the Product Manager to clarify desired outcomes and refine backlog items
- Partners with the Project Manager on scope, dependencies, and requirement-change communication
- Collaborates with Developers and QA/Testing to clarify expected behavior and validate acceptance criteria
- Engages stakeholders and subject-matter experts to confirm requirements

---

## Release / Operations Owner (or SRE)

### Role Summary
The Release / Operations Owner coordinates operational readiness and helps ensure changes can be deployed, monitored, and supported safely.

### Responsibilities
- Define or coordinate operational readiness checks and deployment requirements
- Confirm monitoring, alerting, rollback, and mitigation plans are in place
- Coordinate release readiness and operational handoffs
- Help triage production issues and feed operational learnings into follow-up work

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with the Engineering Lead and Developers on reliability, deployment, and observability needs
- Coordinates with QA/Testing on release verification and smoke tests
- Works with the Project Manager on release timing and readiness risks, and with Customer Support/Success on incident and support handoffs
- Aligns with the Product Manager on customer impact and release outcomes

---

## Customer Support / Customer Success Representative

### Role Summary
The Customer Support / Customer Success Representative brings customer-facing needs into delivery and helps prepare teams to support changes after release.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring customer feedback, support trends, and known pain points
- Identify customer communication, documentation, and training needs
- Prepare support guidance and help route post-release questions or issues
- Report customer impact and adoption signals to the project team

### Interaction with Other Roles
- Partners with the Product Manager to connect customer feedback to prioritization and success measures
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on stakeholder updates and readiness activities
- Works with the Release/Operations Owner on support handoffs and incident communication
- Shares customer-facing questions and acceptance feedback with Developers and QA/Testing through agreed channels

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Adapt role assignments to project context; one person may cover multiple roles, and not every project needs every persona.
